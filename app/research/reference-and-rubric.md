## Draft reviewer checklist

For each anonymized sample, the reviewer needs the target, final image, and a replay of its ordered events. Use the approved character specification and record one of **pass**, **fail**, **unclear**, or **not applicable** for each supported check. Multiple failures may apply to one sample. The criteria, acceptable variations, and any thresholds are pending expert review.
