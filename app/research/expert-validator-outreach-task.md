# Task: Find an expert Baybayin validator for the pilot

## Goal

Identify and contact a qualified person who can review and approve the writing rules used for the first Baybayin validator pilot. The initial scope is the unmodified characters `ba` and `ka` under the intended Pinagyamang Baybayin convention.

## What to look for

Find candidates with demonstrated expertise in Baybayin writing, teaching, scholarship, or standards work. Prefer someone who can explain and document their chosen convention, rather than only provide a general endorsement.

Possible sources include Baybayin educators, cultural or language organizations, university faculty, recognized practitioners, and organizations connected with Filipino writing-system standards.

## Deliverables

For each candidate, provide:

1. Name, organization or role, and a public professional link or other verifiable source.
2. Why they are qualified for this pilot.
3. The Baybayin convention or teaching approach they use, if known.
4. A contact route suitable for an initial research inquiry.
5. Their apparent availability or willingness to review the pilot, if contact has been made.

Do not publish private contact details in shared project files. Keep any non-public contact information in the team’s approved private channel.

## What the expert will be asked to do

The expert will review the supplied *Pagsulat ng Baybayin* diagrams and the physical-book source when available. For `ba` and `ka`, they will be asked to confirm:

- the intended character form and allowed handwriting variations;
- the meaning of the numbered diagram marks;
- stroke start and end points, direction, order, and pen lifts;
- whether alternate formations are acceptable;
- what should count as incorrect or unclear;
- the source or convention supporting each decision.

They may then draw several approved reference examples using the development app. Each recording is saved as JSON and linked to the approved character specification.

## Materials to provide the expert

- [Draft `ba` and `ka` pilot specifications](ba-ka-pilot-character-specifications.md)
- [Reference and rubric working notes](reference-and-rubric.md)
- The supplied `Pagsulat ng Baybayin` PDF
- A short demonstration of the drawing-recording prototype

## Definition of done

This task is complete when the team has either:

- one confirmed expert who agrees to review the pilot and a scheduled review session; or
- a ranked shortlist of at least three suitable candidates, with evidence of qualification and a documented outreach status for each.

After review, record the expert’s decisions, source page references, review date, and specification version before using any rules or recordings as validator ground truth.
